Treatment positioning

First answer one question: Is the patient a suitable candidate?

The first commercial step is medical screening — not selling surgery.

Potential discussion points

  • Degenerative or traumatic articular cartilage damage — as stated in hospital materials
  • Patients seeking a specialist opinion on joint-preservation options
  • Patients able to provide current MRI and lower-extremity X-ray

What we will not promise

  • No '100% cure' or money-back medical guarantee
  • No claim that every patient can avoid artificial joint replacement
  • No FDA-approval claim without exact documented wording and approval for Thailand use
